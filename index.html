Around The World

Six Resorts, One Kind of Magic

Disney operates twelve theme parks across six resort destinations on three continents, plus a fleet of cruise ships. Here's the lay of the land — and roughly what to budget.

Florida, USA

Walt Disney World

Bay Lake / Orlando · 4 theme parks + 2 water parks

The largest Disney resort in the world, home to Magic Kingdom, EPCOT, Hollywood Studios, and Animal Kingdom, plus dozens of resort hotels and Disney Springs.

1-day, 1-park ticket~$115–$200
California, USA

Disneyland Resort

Anaheim · 2 theme parks

The original Disneyland, opened in 1955, sits alongside Disney California Adventure. Walkable, classic, and steeped in the park's earliest history.

1-day, 1-park ticket~$104–$224
France

Disneyland Paris

Marne-la-Vallée · 2 theme parks

Europe's Disney resort blends the classic park formula with distinctly European architecture and storytelling touches you won't find elsewhere.

1-day, 1-park ticket~€50–€105
Japan

Tokyo Disney Resort

Urayasu, Chiba · 2 theme parks

Tokyo Disneyland and Tokyo DisneySea (the only DisneySea in the world) are known for immaculate upkeep, standout nighttime shows, and generally lower ticket prices.

1-day, 1-park ticket~$60–$95
China

Hong Kong Disneyland

Lantau Island · 1 theme park

The smallest Disney park, ideal for a relaxed day or a stop on a broader Asia itinerary alongside Tokyo or Shanghai.

1-day ticket~$70–$100
China

Shanghai Disney Resort

Pudong, Shanghai · 1 theme park

The newest Disney park, featuring the largest Disney castle structure built to date and attractions found nowhere else, including a TRON coaster and a Zootopia land.

1-day ticket~$60–$95
On The High Seas

Disney Cruise Line

A fleet of Disney-owned ships sailing the Caribbean, Bahamas, Alaska, and Europe, with Castaway Cay and Lookout Cay as private island stops. Pricing below is per person, for a standard inside stateroom, and varies heavily by ship, season, and how far out you book.

3–5 Night Bahamas / Caribbean
$1,150–$1,900
Sails from Port Canaveral or Fort Lauderdale
7 Night Caribbean / Alaska
$2,500–$4,000+
Best booked 8–12 months ahead for lower fares
10+ Night European / Transatlantic
$3,000–$8,000+
Fewer sailings per year, premium pricing

All prices are approximate 2026 ranges gathered for planning purposes only — Disney and third-party travel sites use dynamic, date-based pricing that shifts constantly with season, cabin category, and demand. Always confirm current rates directly with Disney or a travel advisor before booking.
